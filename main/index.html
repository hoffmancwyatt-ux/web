the third chamber · how a claim proceeds

The Rite

Four operations, in order — and one golden thread through all of them. Scroll, and watch it stitch.

Ielection

Choose your relic

Read its seal, materials, and measurements on its own page. If you need a measurement I haven't listed, send a petition before you claim — I answer quickly.

IIoffering

Make the offering through PayPal

Press Claim and you'll be carried to PayPal with the exact price. Inscribe the relic's seal number in the payment note — this binds the offering to the piece.

IIIbinding

The relic is struck from the catalogue

Within a day of your offering, the piece is marked claimed here for all to see, and confirmation comes to you by mail.

IVsending

Sealed and sent

Your relic ships within 5 days, wrapped and wax-sealed, with tracking. It travels anywhere in the world the couriers dare to go.

Should two offerings arrive for one relic, the later offering is returned in full, with apologies — the first hand holds the claim. Questions of fit, alteration, or return are settled by petition, plainly and fairly. No relic is ever remade.

The Hidden Relic

One garment has no page in the catalogue. It hangs veiled above the altar, and it can be seen only by a visitor who fills the bowl with the day's offering — the right ingredients, in the right order — and puts a flame to it.

the grimoire of the bowl

Ingredients

Every ingredient on the altar is real, and each has been placed in a bowl, a fire, or a temple by someone, somewhere, for a very long time.

Several of these are poisonous, several are sacred to living peoples, and some are controlled by law where you live. This bowl is a game and a history lesson: nothing here is a recipe, and nothing here goes in a cup.
